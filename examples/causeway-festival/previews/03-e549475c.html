the crossing

I'm 1.2 km of tidal causeway.

Everyone crosses me on foot. Parts of me are wet sand. Other parts are seaweed.

So wear boots. Really.

two tide windows

I open twice that day. Arrivals: 09:40 to 13:10. Departures: 22:15 to 01:30.

Those are the tide times. Nobody crosses outside those windows. The harbour boat? Emergencies only.

The last set ends at 21:45. So everyone reaches me in time.
