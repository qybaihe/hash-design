What's Causeway, exactly?

What's Causeway, exactly?

A one-day music festival out on Skerrow Island, happening on Saturday 19 June 2027.

Year four, then: the very first Causeway ran back in 2024.

The Skerrow Island Trust runs the whole thing, and the Trust is a volunteer charity. Any profit goes straight to repairing the sea wall.

When's the causeway open?

When's the causeway open?

Twice that day, and only twice. The windows below are that day's tide times, not a rough guide.

  • 09:40–13:10 Arrivals cross in this window, on foot, over the full 1.2 km.

  • 21:45 The last set ends here, so everyone reaches the causeway in time.

  • 22:15–01:30 Departures cross back in this window, again going by that day's tide.

  • Nobody crosses outside those windows. The harbour boat is for emergencies only, not a late lift home.

What's to eat, and what to pack?

What's to eat, and what to pack?

  • Food comes from two island kitchens, and both cash and card are fine.

  • No single-use plastic on the island. Bring a cup, or buy an enamel one for £6.

  • Wear boots. Parts of the causeway are wet sand and seaweed, so trainers won't enjoy it.
