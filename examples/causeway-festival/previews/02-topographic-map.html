Cause·way/ˈkɔːz.weɪ/n.

  1. 1 a one-day music festival on Skerrow Island, Saturday 19 June 2027; the fourth, first held in 2024.
  2. 2 the 1.2 km tidal path by which the island is reached, on foot.

the causeway opens for arrivals at 09:40 and closes behind them at 13:10.

→ see tide window, legend

legendn.

1 the key to a map. 2 on this sheet, the day itself: every sign below stands for a fact of Causeway, Saturday 19 June 2027. Sections keep the survey’s usual order, relabelled.

01

Crossing

roads & paths

causeway n. 1 the 1.2 km path over tidal sand to Skerrow Island, walked on foot. 2 open for arrivals 09:40–13:10 and for departures 22:15–01:30. → see tide window

tidal sands n. pl. wet sand and seaweed, underfoot on parts of the causeway. Wear boots.

not
shown

harbour boat n. for emergencies only; not a crossing. Nobody crosses outside the tide windows.

02

Tide

water

tide window n. the time the causeway is open, from that day’s tide times. 1 arrivals 09:40–13:10. 2 departures 22:15–01:30. Shore line solid; low-water mark dotted.

water line n. drawn at even steps out from the shore. Water names on this sheet are set in italic.

last set n. ends at 21:45, so that everyone reaches the causeway in time.
