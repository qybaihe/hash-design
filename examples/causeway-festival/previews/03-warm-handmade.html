Hours of the crossing

that day’s tide times
The way over 09:40–13:10 arrivals, onto the island
Last set ends 21:45 so that everyone reaches the causeway in time
The way home 22:15–01:30 departures, off the island

Outside these windows nobody crosses. The island is reached on foot over 1.2 km of tidal causeway, and the tide sets the times.

Favourable

  • Boots.

    Parts of the causeway are wet sand and seaweed.

  • A cup of your own.

    Or buy an enamel one on the island.

  • An early start.

    The way over opens at 09:40 and closes at 13:10.

Unfavourable

  • Single-use plastic.

    There is none on the island.

  • Crossing out of hours.

    Nobody crosses outside the windows above.

  • Waiting on the boat.

    The harbour boat is for emergencies only.

  • Your good shoes.

    See: boots.
