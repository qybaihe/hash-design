General notes

  1. Tickets on sale 1 Mar 2027: adult £54, ages 12–17 £27, under 12 free (item 1).
  2. Island capacity 900 people. The Boathouse holds 220, indoors (item 6); the Field is outdoors (item 7).
  3. 21:45 — last set ends, so everyone reaches the causeway before the departures window closes.
  4. No single-use plastic on the island. Bring a cup, or buy an enamel one for £6 (item 4).
  5. Food from two island kitchens (item 5). Cash and card.
  6. Organised by the Skerrow Island Trust, a volunteer charity. Profits go to repairing the sea wall (item 8).
  7. Fourth year of the festival; first held in 2024 (rev A).

Do not scale from this sheet. Row D alone is drawn to scale, in time.
