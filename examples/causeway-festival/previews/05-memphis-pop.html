Part two · getting there, getting back

So how does the day work?

QHow do I get onto the island?

A

You walk. It’s a 1.2 km tidal causeway, so it’s only open when the tide allows. On Saturday 19 June that means two windows:

Arrivals09:40–13:10
Departures22:15–01:30

Those are that day’s tide times.

QAnd if I miss the window?

A

Then you don’t cross. Nobody crosses outside those windows. [pause]

The harbour boat is for emergencies only.

QWhat does a ticket cost?

A
  • Adult£54
  • Ages 12–17£27
  • Under 12Free

On sale 1 March 2027.

QHow many of us?

A 900

That’s capacity. Nine hundred people, one island.

QWhat should I wear?

A Boots.

[laughs] We mean it. Parts of the causeway are wet sand and seaweed.

QPlastic cups at the bar?

A

No single-use plastic on the island. Bring a cup, or buy an enamel one for £6.

QIs there food?

A

From two island kitchens. Cash and card.

QIs this new?

A 4th

Our fourth year. We first held it in 2024.
