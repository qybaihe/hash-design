Sluice · version 0.9.2Room 3

Notes on the works

Notes run 1–9. Each note names its plate. Return to the plate by its number.

  1. 1
    Pl. 1 · Function

    Receive webhooks at a public URL. Store every delivery. Forward each delivery to your local or internal service.

  2. 2
    Pl. 2 · Distribution

    Install one static binary, about 11 MB. Targets: Linux, macOS and Windows, each on amd64 and arm64. Language: Go.

  3. 3
    Pl. 3 · Storage

    Keep all storage in a single SQLite file. Run no Redis, no Postgres, no queue.

  4. 4
    Pl. 6 · Retries

    Sluice retries a failed forward with exponential backoff. Retrying stops after 72 hours.

  5. 5
    Pl. 7 · Provenance

    Ines Okafor started Sluice in 2023, after losing a weekend of payment webhooks. To replay any stored delivery, run sluice replay <delivery-id>.

  6. 6
    Pl. 8 · Verification

    Sluice verifies HMAC-SHA256 signatures. It rejects deliveries older than 5 minutes.

  7. 7
    Pl. 10 · Retention
    Caution

    Sluice keeps deliveries for 30 days by default. Change the period with --retain.

  8. 8
    Pl. 11 · Measurement
    Note

    1,800 deliveries per second on a 2-vCPU VM, in the project’s own benchmark. The benchmark carries no date; the plate reads n.d.

  9. 9
    Pl. 12 · Absence

    The plate is left blank. Sluice has no hosted service, no account, no telemetry.
