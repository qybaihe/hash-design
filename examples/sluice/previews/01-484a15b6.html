Sluice — a self-hosted inbox for webhooks

I hold your webhooks.

You get a public URL. I store every delivery. Then I forward it. To your local service. Or an internal one.

Install Sluice

I am one binary.

One static binary. About 11 MB.

Linux, macOS, Windows. amd64 and arm64.

I keep one SQLite file.

  • No Redis.
  • No Postgres.
  • No queue to run.
install 
$ curl -fsSL https://sluice.example/install.sh | sh
start 
$ sluice serve --forward http://localhost:3000

I keep every delivery.

retry
A forward fails. I retry. Exponential backoff. Up to 72 hours.
72 hours
replay
Any stored delivery. I send it again. sluice replay <delivery-id>
verify
I check HMAC-SHA256 signatures. Older than 5 minutes? Rejected.
5 minutes
search
My web UI: port 8081. Search by path. By header. By body text.
retain
I keep deliveries 30 days. That is the default. Change it: --retain
30 days
speed
1,800 deliveries per second. A 2-vCPU VM. The project's own benchmark.
1,800/sec
