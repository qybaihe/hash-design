Aim: catch every webhook, keep it, pass it on.

  1. 1.1

    Observed: Sluice gives webhooks a public URL, stores every delivery, then forwards it to a local or internal service.

  2. 1.2

    One static binary, about 11 MB. Built for Linux, macOS and Windows, on amd64 and arm64 (table 2.2).

  3. 1.3

    Storage: a single SQLite file. Also to run: Redis Postgres a queue nothing I.O.

  4. 1.4

    No hosted service. No account. No telemetry.

  5. 1.5

    Origin, 2023: Ines Okafor started it after losing a weekend of payment webhooks.

No.3

14 Oct 2026

One delivery, followed through time

  1. 3.1

    At arrival: HMAC-SHA256 signature verified. Deliveries older than 5 min are rejected.

  2. 3.2

    Forwarded to the local or internal service. A failed forward is retried with exponential backoff, for up to 72 h.

  3. 3.3

    Any stored delivery can be replayed: sluice replay <delivery-id>.

  4. 3.4

    Kept 30 days by default (--retain to change). Found again in the web UI on port 8081, by path, header or body text.
