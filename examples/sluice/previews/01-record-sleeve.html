Notes on this pressing

Sluice gives you a public URL for webhooks, stores every delivery, and forwards it to your local or internal service.

Started in 2023 by Ines Okafor, after losing a weekend of payment webhooks.

To play

  1. 01Installcurl -fsSL https://sluice.example/install.sh | sh
  2. 02Startsluice serve --forward http://localhost:3000
  3. 03Replaysluice replay <delivery-id>
